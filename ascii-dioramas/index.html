About

Started in November 2026 as a side project inside Curiositas, after seeing ASCII diorama demos circulating online—in particular the work by @HelloVyom and @dhruvtwt_ (repo).

Each scene is a single main.js that draws instanced geometry (grass, flowers, trees, props) into a half-float render target, then runs it through a glyph shader that picks characters by surface class and brightness, followed by a tilt-shift bokeh pass. The camera sweeps slowly back and forth; a small creature walks through.
