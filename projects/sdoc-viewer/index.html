About

unsdocx

A viewer for Samsung Notes files — the .sdocx format (and older .sdoc) that Samsung Notes exports. Open a file to read its handwriting, browse embedded images and audio, and inspect the raw archive.

Everything runs in your browser. The file is read locally and never uploaded — no server, no tracking, no external requests.

What it shows

  • Pages — handwriting and imported images, rendered to SVG by the ink engine. Hover a page to save it as a PNG.
  • Media — images and audio embedded in the note.
  • Structure — the internal Zip entries; click one to inspect text/hex.
  • Raw — the fully parsed document as JSON.

How it works

An .sdocx is a Zip container, read here with JSZip. Pages are decoded and drawn by @twango/sdocx, a reverse-engineered WebAssembly parser, which rasterizes each page to SVG — ink, imported slides, and photos together. It's best-effort and reverse-engineered, so a page may still come out incomplete. That's a parser limitation, not lost data — keep the original file.
