A workbench for things I build to understand: small web experiments, AI toys, cryptography demos, and interactive explanations. Each one lives in its own folder, with its own toolchain — some plain HTML, some Rust compiled to WASM, some whatever the idea needed. Most are unfinished. That is the point.

The name is Latin for curiosity; in Kannada it would be ಕುತೂಹಲ (kutūhala), which is closer to the feeling — the itch to poke at something until it makes sense.11 Longer write-ups, when a project earns one, go on the blog, Technica Insania.
